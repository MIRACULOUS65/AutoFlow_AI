# Finance Reporting Policy

## Monthly Closing
The monthly closing checklist is completed by the fifth business day of each month.

## Weekly Reporting
Finance receives the approved weekly sales report and reconciles it against ledger totals.
Reports that fail reconciliation are returned to the sales operations team.

## Distribution
Financial summaries are distributed only to the finance distribution group after approval.

## Retention
Financial reports are retained for seven years per the retention policy.
