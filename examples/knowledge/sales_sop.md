# Weekly Sales Reporting Standard Operating Procedure

## Purpose
This SOP defines how the approved weekly sales report is prepared and distributed.

## Process
1. Sales data is pulled from the approved data warehouse every Monday morning.
2. The analyst compiles the figures using the standard sales report template.
3. Totals are reconciled against the prior week before the report is finalized.
4. The completed report is reviewed by the sales lead.
5. After approval, the report is emailed to the finance distribution group.

## Approval
No weekly sales report may be sent externally without explicit sales-lead approval.

## Notes
The weekly sales reporting process is owned by the Sales operations team.
